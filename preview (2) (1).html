— Section 02 · Market Research —

The numbers behind the opportunity.

Every claim here is grounded in 2026 data. We size the market, locate the growth curve, identify regional density, and confirm that conversational commerce is a once-in-a-decade software wave.

Routing, SEO & UX Rules

— Country Detection —

Use IP or browser locale to suggest the right country site, but do not force it. Show a soft banner: “Looks like you’re in Brazil. Continue in Português?” Remember the choice in a cookie/local storage.

— Language Switcher —

Header should always show country + language: “Brazil · Português”, “India · English”, “UAE · العربية / English”. Allow users to switch country and language anytime.

— Hreflang —

Every localized page needs hreflang tags and a canonical URL. Example: the Brazil pricing page should reference pt-BR, global English, and x-default versions.

— Local Slugs —

For SEO pages, use local-language slugs where search demand is local. Example: /br/precos and /br/recuperacao-de-carrinho-abandonado are better than English slugs.

— Currency & Billing —

Show local currency on localized pricing pages. Meta charges should be explained separately with examples. Do not make users calculate USD manually.

— Content Quality —

Local pages should be written by native or near-native speakers. Translation is acceptable for docs, but homepage, pricing, competitor and SEO pages need local copywriting.

— Competitor Conquest —

Win comparison searches without sounding cheap or aggressive.

Purpose: capture “alternative to X” searches.

— Comparison Pages —

Purpose: capture “X vs Y” searches.

Purpose: convert frustrated customers.

— Legal / Brand Safety Rule —

Competitor pages must be factual, respectful and maintained. Do not make unsupported claims like “60% markup” unless sourced internally. Use “best for / not best for” language rather than attacks.

— Technical SEO System —

The infrastructure must make localization crawlable, fast, and unambiguous.

🔗

Hreflang Clusters

Each equivalent page must reference every language/country version, including itself and x-default. Example: /pricing/, /br/precos/, /mx/precios/, /id/harga/.

📌

Canonical Discipline

Every localized page should self-canonicalize. Do not canonicalize Brazil Portuguese pricing back to global English pricing.

🗺️

XML Sitemaps

Create separate sitemaps by country and page type: sitemap-br.xml, sitemap-mx.xml, sitemap-integrations.xml, sitemap-templates.xml.

⚡

Performance

Core pages must be fast on mobile. Compress images, avoid heavy scripts, lazy-load non-critical sections and keep landing pages lightweight.

🧩

Schema Markup

Use Organization, SoftwareApplication, FAQ, Breadcrumb, Product, Review where appropriate, and Article schema for resources.

🧭

Internal Linking

Every support page links to a commercial page. Every commercial page links to pricing, demo, use cases, trust and relevant comparison pages.
